This infographic, titled "Navigating the Digital World: A Student's Guide," serves as a comprehensive roadmap for developing essential digital competencies.
It breaks down the digital journey into three core pillars: defining digital literacy through skills like critical thinking and ethical creation, identifying useful
digital tools for productivity and research, and outlining safe internet practices to protect personal data. By emphasizing responsible technology use and security
measures—such as recognizing phishing and using secure HTTPS connections—the guide aims to empower students to communicate, collaborate, and navigate the online
landscape effectively and safely.
